Results (Draft with Programmatically Inserted Values)

The cross-lagged panel network comprised 9 nodes, yielding 52 non-zero cross-lagged edges out of 72 possible directed edges. Autoregressive edges ranged from 0.28 to 0.45.
The three strongest cross-lagged edges were: T1 'Sleep problems' → T2 'Fatigue' (β = 0.14), T1 'Feeling left out' → T2 'No one understands me' (β = 0.13), and T1 'No one understands me' → T2 'Fatigue' (β = 0.11).
The sum of edge weights from Loneliness to Depression was 0.56, whereas that from Depression to Loneliness was 0.08.
The node with the highest out-expected influence was 'No one understands me' (out-EI = 0.61), and the node with the highest in-expected influence was 'Fatigue' (in-EI = 0.40).
Case-dropping bootstrap revealed that the CS-coefficient was 0.20 for out-EI and 0.52 for in-EI; centrality rankings with CS < 0.25 were not interpreted. The CS for in-EI was close to the 0.5 threshold (marginally stable; changing the random seed may shift it to an adjacent grid level), and its rank order should be interpreted with caution.
In sensitivity analyses, the edge-weight correlations between the primary analysis and networks estimated via lambda.1se, including covariates, and across T2→T3 were 0.67, 0.997, 0.64, respectively.

Full Procedure Sensitivity Analysis. In accordance with the full procedure recommended by Wysocki et al. (2025), nodewise LASSO regressions were first conducted separately for the T1→T2 and T2→T3 lags. The 67 cross-lagged paths non-zero in either lag were entered into a path model (the remainder fixed to zero) and re-estimated without penalization in lavaan (cross-sectional residual covariances within wave, FIML). Nested testing of cross-time invariance constraints yielded Δχ²(76) = 84.69, p = 0.232, and both AIC and BIC favored the constrained model; therefore, the constrained model specifying equal paths across lags was retained as the final model (χ²(167) = 249.39, CFI = 0.987, TLI = 0.975, RMSEA = 0.025, SRMR = 0.027). Pruning at p < .01 retained 16 cross-lagged edges (3 Loneliness→Depression, 2 Depression→Loneliness, 11 within-construct). Of the 52 non-zero cross-lagged edges in the primary analysis, 16 were retained in the full procedure; among the 10 strongest edges in the primary analysis, 8 were retained (those not retained were: T1 'No one understands me' → T2 'Fatigue' (primary analysis β = 0.11; full procedure β = 0.03, p = 0.264); T1 'Worthlessness' → T2 'Fatigue' (primary analysis β = 0.09; full procedure β = 0.04, p = 0.131)). Following pruning, the sum of edge weights from Loneliness to Depression was 0.30, and from Depression to Loneliness was -0.04 (compared to 0.56 and 0.08 in the primary analysis).

(Methodological Caveat: Observed-score CLPN conflates between-person and within-person effects; findings reflect predictive relationships rather than causal processes.)
